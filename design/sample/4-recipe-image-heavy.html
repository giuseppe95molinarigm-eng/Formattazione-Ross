Rice Pilaf Mix
Butter, this jar, and a pot of water: dinner’s rice side is basically done before the rest of the meal even starts.
Yield1 batch (about ¾ cup dry mix), makes pilaf for 3 to 4 servings
Ingredients
  • 2 tablespoons orzo pasta
  • 1 tablespoon dried onion flakes
  • 1 teaspoon bouillon
  • ¼ teaspoon garlic powder
  • ½ cup rice
  • ⅛ teaspoon turmeric
Instructions
  1. Combine the orzo, dried onion flakes, bouillon, garlic powder, rice, and turmeric in a small container.
  2. Store in an airtight container until ready to use.
Storage
Store in an airtight container in the pantry for up to 6 months.
