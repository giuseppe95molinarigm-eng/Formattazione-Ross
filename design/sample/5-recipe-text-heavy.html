Chicken Fajita Rice Bowl Mix
This one is for those nights when chicken, peppers, and rice are already on the grocery list. Having the rice and fajita seasoning together means you can cook the components without making a separate seasoning blend while everything else is going.
Placeholder — fajita rice bowl, overhead
Servings6
Serving Sizeabout ⅓ cup dry rice and seasoning mix, for one rice bowl.
What’s in the Jar?
  • 2 cups of instant white rice
  • 2 tablespoons chili powder
  • 2 teaspoons paprika
  • 2 teaspoons ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
What to Add
  • Cooked chicken
  • Bell peppers
  • Water
Instructions
  1. Take a bowl and add all the ingredients, such as the instant rice, chili powder, paprika, cumin, garlic and onion powder, oregano, salt, and pepper, to it.
  2. Mix all these things until the seasoning is evenly distributed with the rice thoroughly.
  3. Transfer the prepared pre-mix to a clean container and seal it tightly to keep it airtight.
  4. Then, label the jar for future ease with the name and date.
  5. When you want to prepare, use about ⅓ cup dry mix per bowl. Add cooked chicken, sliced peppers, and water when preparing.
Nutrition (per serving)
Calories: 216 kcal, Protein: 4.7g, Carbs: 46.5g, Fat: 1g
Storage
Store the mix jar tightly sealed in a cool, dry pantry. Keep it stored for up to 6 months. Keep the jar away from the stove so the spices aren’t exposed to heat and steam.
